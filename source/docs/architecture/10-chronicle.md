# 10 - The chronicle

The chronicle is not a report. It is a healing surface.

## What it is

A journal the coach writes. Not the user.

- Weekly entry. Sunday. One paragraph.
- Block entry. When arc transitions.
- Season entry. Every 4-6 arcs or 6-12 months.
- Comeback entry. When COMEBACK arc resolves.

## What it is not

- Not generated on demand. Written continuously.
- Not a summary of charts. Charts serve the narrative.
- Not a list of numbers. Numbers only when they carry meaning.
- Not flattering. Not shaming. Honest.

## How it writes

Every session appends a line to the current week. At end of week, the coach turns lines into a paragraph. At end of block, into a summary. At end of season, into a reassessment.

The user never asks for it. It is always there.

## The five healing moves in every entry

1. Name the drift.
2. Explain the drift.
3. Place it in time.
4. Offer a choice.
5. Close with a real sentence.

## Hallucination constraint

The chronicle cannot invent. Every claim entailed by the log. Generic filler carries a strong penalty.

C* = argmax [ Coverage + ToneFit - Hallucination - Platitude ]

## What the chronicle is for

The user scrolls back three months and smiles. Not because data looks good. Because they recognize their own training in the words.

A diary, not a report.
