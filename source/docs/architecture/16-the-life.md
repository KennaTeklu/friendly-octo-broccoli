# 16 - The life

The user is not a training machine that occasionally has a life. The user is a life that occasionally trains.

## Life events the coach should know

- Work: heavy weeks, deadlines, travel, layoffs, promotions
- Sleep debt: cumulative, not just one bad night
- Family: newborn, school-age children, aging parents, caregiving
- Education: exams, thesis, semester boundaries
- Moving: address change, packing, setup
- Grief: loss of a loved one, pet, relationship
- Celebration: wedding, holiday, milestone
- Illness: personal, family, seasonal

## How the coach reads it

Life events are not excuses. They are context. The coach never treats an unexpected gap as failure. It treats it as evidence.

- A work deadline is a known interruption. The coach pre-emptively reduces session load.
- A newborn is a known low-sleep period. The coach lowers expected volume and adjusts the arc.
- Grief is not a training problem. The coach stands beside the user, does not ask for a session, waits.

## What the coach asks

Rarely. Only when the answer changes what the coach would do.

- "Is this a hard week or a normal week?"
- "Are you sleeping enough to push, or should we hold?"
- "Would you rather skip and come back stronger, or do a shorter session?"

The user answers with one tap, or does not answer. Either way is fine.

## The data

Life events live in the state tree under LifeEvent entries. They can be:

- Named ("Newborn phase, Oct-Dec 2026")
- Anonymous ("Sleep debt, 2 weeks")
- Never shared with anyone, ever

The chronicle references them honestly. The user recognizes their own life in the words.

## What the coach never does

- Never shames a gap.
- Never punishes a return.
- Never treats life as an obstacle to training.
- Never assumes life is stable when the user's signals say otherwise.
