# 25 - Philosophy in practice

The belief from 13-philosophy has to show on every surface. This file says how.

## Today

- The card declares before it prescribes.
- Chips show components and muscles. No numbers without meaning.
- Coach sentence is short and specific.
- Coach, go / Change. Not a form. A conversation.
- No streak counter on this screen. Streaks belong to the user, not the surface.

## Session

- One tap starts. One tap logs a set. One tap finishes.
- The coach may react live. Rarely. Only when the reaction matters.
- No mid-set popups.
- RPE is one slider, not a form.
- The end-of-session note is one line, not a paragraph.

## Chronicle

- Written continuously. Never generated on demand.
- Honest. Names the drift before it explains it.
- Short. Paragraph, not page.
- The user's own words quoted back when they help.
- No flattery. No shaming.

## Goals

- The user speaks. The coach listens.
- Per-muscle intent is one dropdown per muscle, not a form.
- Preview before save. Always.
- No "submit." Saving is a conversation.

## You

- Settings are named after what they do, not what they are.
- Export is one button. It works. It never fails.
- Privacy is documented in plain sentences. Not legalese.
- Legal is available. It is not required reading.
- The exit is designed. It is not hidden.

## What the surfaces never do

- Never broadcast. Motivation is not a design.
- Never shame. Not with red numbers, not with "you lost," not with silence that stings.
- Never assume. The user's philosophy, permission, and context are read, never guessed.
- Never forget. The chronicle remembers the story even when the dossier releases the details.
- Never overstay. When the user is done, the app recedes.

## The test

Read any screen to the user. Ask: does this feel like a coach, or like a machine? If machine, rewrite.
