# 28 - The five surfaces

The user does not need ten screens. They need five questions answered.

## 1. Today - What does the coach want me to do right now?

The card stack. Every exercise card shows the prescription, the component chips, the muscle chips, and one sentence in the coach's voice. This is where the coach speaks.

## 2. Session - Let me do it.

The active workout. Each exercise is a card you tap into. You log the set. The coach may react live. There is no "submit." There is only "done."

## 3. Chronicle - How am I doing?

Not charts. The story. Weekly paragraphs, block summaries, season entries, each with a name. Charts may appear, but they serve the narrative.

## 4. Goals - What do I want?

Where the user speaks to the coach about intent. Per-muscle priorities. Per-exercise overrides. Session-level feel. The tuning dialogue lives here, extended to the whole body.

## 5. You - Who are you to me?

Settings, reframed. Identity, export, sync, accessibility, legal, about. Everything that is about the relationship rather than the training.

## Every other screen

A sub-screen of one of these five.

- Library is under Today.
- Progress is under Chronicle.
- Tuning is under Goals.
- Backup is under You.

## The tab bar

A compass, not a directory. Five icons. One is active. The rest are quiet.

## What a surface never does

- Never opens a new app.
- Never surprises.
- Never hides what just happened.
- Never takes more than one tap to reach.
