# 22 - Permission

Who authorized the coach to decide? On what terms?

## The permission model

At onboarding, the user is not just asked what they want. They are asked how they want to be coached.

Three axes:

**Axis 1 - Speaking style.**
- Tell me what to do.
- Ask me what I want, then tell me.
- Suggest, and let me choose.
- Ask me, then do what I say.

**Axis 2 - Pushing.**
- Push me hard. I want to be challenged.
- Push me, but stop when I say stop.
- Hold me back. I overtrain when unchecked.
- Just guide. Do not push.

**Axis 3 - Permission scope.**
- You can decide everything.
- You can decide sessions; I decide phases.
- You can decide within a session; I decide everything else.
- You can decide nothing; just answer my questions.

## Why three axes

Two users who both want to be pushed may want different speaking styles. Two users who both want to be told may want different permission scopes. The three axes are orthogonal.

## Permission in motion

Permission can change:

- By arc (peak phases may want tighter permission; base phases looser)
- By phase (discovery wants more explanation; specialization less)
- By event (after an injury, the coach gets tighter permission; after a long break, the same)

The user can update permission at any time. Permission changes are announced in the chronicle.

## What the coach never does

- Never exceeds granted permission.
- Never assumes permission from previous phases carries over without confirmation.
- Never treats permission as permanent.
- Never shames a change of permission.
