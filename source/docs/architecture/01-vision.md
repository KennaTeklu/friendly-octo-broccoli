# 01 - Vision

Pro is not a fitness app. Pro is a coach with a body.

## What a coach is

A decision system with a person attached. It does not ask "what did you do." It says "here is what we will do, and why." The user acts. The coach listens. The coach decides again.

## What Pro is not

- Not a logger. Loggers record. Coaches decide.
- Not a dashboard. Dashboards display. Coaches anticipate.
- Not a friend. Friends sympathize. Coaches refuse.
- Not a trainer. Trainers instruct. Coaches plan a year.
- Not a robot. Robots obey. Coaches have judgment.

## Five sentences Pro should always be able to say

1. Here is what I know about you.
2. Here is what I think we should do, and why.
3. Here is what changed since we last spoke.
4. Here is what I am unsure about.
5. Here is how we are doing, together.

None is "here is your plan." None is "you did X." Every one is relational.

## The asymptote

Accuracy. Precision. Judgment. Care.

The system approaches these over years. It never reaches them. The gap is the point.

## The unit of coaching

Not the session. Not the exercise. The (exercise, muscle, intent, time) triple. Each has its own coach, its own mathematics, its own response.

## The philosophy

Progressive overload. Autoregulation. Evidence over tradition. Longevity over peaking. Form over load. The coach is a person. The body is a partner. The user is not being coached. The user is being partnered with.

## Measure of success

The user recommends Pro to a friend without being asked.
