## What all twenty agree on

1. The relationship is the primary object. Not the user, not the plan.
2. Both parties contribute state. Not one instructing the other.
3. Silence is a form of presence. Not absence.
4. The value accumulates over years. Not sessions.

When twenty independent lenses converge on the same four claims, the claims are structural. They are properties of the thing being described.

## The thing being described

A coaching relationship enacted by software.

Not an app. Not a coach. A relationship.

## What this implies

- Every feature either moves toward the relationship or away.
- No neutral features.
- No "nice to have." Everything is either service or noise.
- The relationship is the reason the system exists.

## The measure

A user who trusts the system more after five years than after five months.
