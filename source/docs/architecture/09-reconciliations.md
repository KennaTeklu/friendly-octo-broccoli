# 09 - The four reconciliations

Four views of the same person drift apart over years.

| View | Holds | Fails when |
|---|---|---|
| Expectation | What the user thinks should happen | Outruns reality |
| Prescription | What the coach programmed | Assumes a body not there |
| Reality | What was done, felt, responded | Gets ignored |
| Story | The narrative the user tells | Flatters or shames |

Coaching heals the drift. Not motivation. Reconciliation.

## The mechanism

The stimulus field can be read from four directions: intent, exercise, response, arc. Four queries. One field. The field holds them consistent.

## Expectation <-> Reality

User thinks 100 kg bench. Field says 92.5. Chronicle places the gap in time.

"You remember 100. That was a peak, not a baseline. You are 92.5 today. Peak starts in 4 weeks. Expect 100 by week 3 of Peak. This is not regression. This is the shape of a training year."

## Prescription <-> Reality

Coach wrote 4x8 at 75%. User did 4x6 at 70% at RPE 9. Next prescription: 4x6 at 70%.

"The plan called for 8s. Your body gave 6s. That is not failure. That is the plan reading you."

## Story <-> Log

Chronicle says steady build. Log shows two missed sessions and a PR.

"Two sessions missed, one PR hit. That is a hard week, not a great one. But a PR in a hard week means something. You showed up twice and moved the bar."

## User intent <-> Coach intent

User wants bigger glutes. Field says hamstrings respond fast.

"You want bigger glutes. Your body grows glutes slowly. Your hamstrings grow fast. If glutes are the goal, we add hip thrusts and accept slow progress. If you want bigger legs overall, we lean into hamstrings. Which one?"

## The five healing moves

1. Name the drift.
2. Explain the drift.
3. Place it in time.
4. Offer a choice.
5. Close with a real sentence.

Not "you got this." Something true about what just happened.

## Why not motivation

Motivation is broadcast. Reconciliation is negotiation with specific data. To this user, on this day, in this arc: here is what you expected, here is what is true, here is the bridge.
