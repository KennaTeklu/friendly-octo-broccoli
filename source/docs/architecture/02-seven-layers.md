# 02 - The seven layers

Every screen, every decision, every notification serves one of two verbs: speaking or listening. The app is a conversation with a coach that happens to also be software.

## The stack

1. Guardian. Safety, privacy, correctness. Can veto.
2. Lifecycle. Arcs, seasons, comebacks, onboarding.
3. Decision. The 42 coaches. Reads state, decides, writes back.
4. Data. Dossier, arc state, chronicle, session log, tuning, identity, export.
5. Composition. Screens, cards, sheets, dialogs.
6. Interaction. Input, output, feedback.
7. The field. The substrate beneath Decision.

## Flow

Information flows up. Constraints flow down. The Decision layer reads and writes Data. The field is the shared language between coaches and sessions.

## Inside the coach column

- Overall Coach (assembly).
- 11 Component Coaches (one per training component).
- ~30 Exercise Coaches (one per movement family).
- The Chronicle (narrator, healer).

## Outside the coach column, inside the brain

- The Dossier (memory).
- The Arc machine (timeline).
- The Envelope system (bounds).
- RPE calibration and ACWR.
- The Rules library.

## Outside the brain

Library (1413 exercises). Session logger. Charts. Chronicle view. Notifications. Identity key. Export contract.

The brain is small. The body is large. The brain is where the intelligence lives.
