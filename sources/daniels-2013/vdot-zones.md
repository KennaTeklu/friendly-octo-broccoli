# Daniels VDOT Training Zones

Source: Jack Daniels, Daniels' Running Formula (3rd edition, 2013).
Chapter: Training Intensities.

VDOT is a measure of running fitness derived from race performance.
Training zones are percentages of VDOT pace or heart rate reserve.

## Table

| Zone | Name          | %VDOT pace | %HRmax | Duration    | Purpose |
|------|--------------|-----------|--------|-------------|---------|
| E    | Easy         | 59-74%     | 65-79% | 20-60 min   | Aerobic base |
| M    | Marathon     | 75-84%     | 80-90% | 40-120 min  | Marathon pace |
| T    | Threshold    | 88-92%     | 88-92% | 20-40 min   | Lactate threshold |
| I    | Interval     | 95-100%    | 93-97% | 3-5 min rep | VO2max |
| R    | Repetition   | 105-120%   | 95-100%| 30-90 sec   | Speed/economy |

## Polarized model

80% of training in Zone E (Easy). 20% in Zones T/I/R.
This is the Seiler polarized model applied to Daniels zones.

## Notes

- Prescription carries distance and pace. No reps, no sets, no load.
- Pace is expressed as VDOT percentage or minutes per mile/km.
- For a novice runner: Zone E only for 4-6 weeks before introducing T.
