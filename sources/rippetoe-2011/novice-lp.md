# Starting Strength Novice Linear Progression

Source: Mark Rippetoe, Starting Strength: Basic Barbell Training, 3rd edition (2011).
Chapter: Programming. The Novice Linear Progression.

## Table

| Phase    | Sets | Reps | %1RM (start) | %1RM (week 4) | Rest |
|----------|------|------|-------------|----------------|------|
| Novice   | 3    | 5    | 65          | 75             | 3-5 min |
| Advanced Novice | 3 | 5 | 75 | 82 | 3-5 min |
| Intermediate | 5 | 3 | 80 | 85 | 3-5 min |

## Progression rule

Add 5 lbs per session for upper body. Add 10 lbs per session for lower body.
When the user fails to complete all reps across all sets, deload 10% and
continue.

## Notes

- Novice = first 3-6 months of training. Linear progression works because
  the trainee adapts between sessions.
- Intermediate = when linear progression stalls, switch to 5×5 across or
  weekly periodization.
- Rest: 3-5 minutes for compound lifts. 1-2 minutes for assistance.
