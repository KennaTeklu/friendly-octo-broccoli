# Prilepin's Chart

Source: A.S. Prilepin, 1974. Soviet Weightlifting. Chart transcribed from
Sheiko / multiple secondary sources (e.g. www_strengthandconditioningresearch_com).

The chart prescribes optimal total reps at given %1RM intensity zones.

## Table

| %1RM | Reps per set | Optimal total | Range |
|------|--------------|---------------|-------|
| 70+  | 3-6          | 18            | 12-24 |
| 70-79| 3-6          | 18            | 12-24 |
| 80-89| 2-4          | 15            | 10-20 |
| 90+  | 1-2          | 7             | 4-10  |

## Rest

3-5 minutes between sets for >80% 1RM.
2-3 minutes for 70-79% 1RM.

## Notes

- "Optimal" means the total rep count that produced the best competition results in Prilepin's data.
- The "Range" column is the acceptable range. Below minimum is insufficient stimulus; above maximum risks overtraining.
- This chart applies to compound barbell lifts (squat, deadlift, bench, press, row).
