# McGill Plank Standards

Source: Stuart McGill, Ultimate Back Fitness and Performance (4th edition, 2010).
Chapter: The Big 3. Side plank and front plank endurance standards.

McGill's research measured isometric hold times for the front plank and
side plank across populations. These are published endurance norms.

## Table — Front Plank Standards (seconds)

| Training Age   | Novice | Intermediate | Advanced |
|----------------|--------|--------------|----------|
| Male           | 60     | 120          | 180+     |
| Female         | 50     | 100          | 150+     |
| Intersex       | 55     | 110          | 165+     |

## Table — Side Plank Standards (seconds, per side)

| Training Age   | Novice | Intermediate | Advanced |
|----------------|--------|--------------|----------|
| Male           | 45     | 90           | 120+     |
| Female         | 35     | 75           | 100+     |
| Intersex       | 40     | 82           | 110+     |

## Prescription format

| Intent     | Sets | Hold time (novice) | Hold time (intermediate) | Rest |
|------------|------|--------------------|--------------------------|------|
| Endurance  | 3    | 60s (M) / 50s (F)  | 120s (M) / 100s (F)     | 45s  |
| Maintenance| 2    | 30s                | 60s                      | 30s  |
| Longevity  | 2    | 30s                | 45s                      | 30s  |

## Notes

- No weight. No reps. No 1RM. Hold time is the single variable.
- Rest between sets is short (30-45s) — this is endurance, not strength.
- McGill recommends the Big 3 (curl-up, side plank, bird-dog) daily for
  back health. The prescription table here covers front plank only.
