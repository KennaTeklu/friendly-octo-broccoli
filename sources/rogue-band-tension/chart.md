# Rogue Band Tension Chart

Source: Rogue Fitness resistance band product specifications (2024).
Based on manufacturer-stated resistance at mid-range stretch.

Resistance bands do not have a fixed weight. The effective load depends
on the degree of stretch. This chart lists the approximate resistance
at the midpoint of the band's stretch range.

## Table

| Colour | Resistance (lbs) | Approx min stretch | Approx max stretch |
|--------|------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Yellow | 5                | 2 lbs              | 15 lbs             |
| Red    | 10               | 5 lbs              | 25 lbs             |
| Green  | 15               | 8 lbs              | 35 lbs             |
| Blue   | 20               | 10 lbs             | 50 lbs             |
| Black  | 25               | 15 lbs             | 60 lbs             |

## Notes

- Band colour IS the load variable. No weight field. No %1RM.
- Effective resistance at full stretch is approximately 2-3x the
  mid-range value listed here.
- For banded lateral raises: the user selects a colour. The prescription
  uses the mid-range resistance as the reference value.
- For banded face pulls: same colour ladder, higher rep range.
